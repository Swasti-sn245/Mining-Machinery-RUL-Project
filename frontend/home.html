Process

How it works.

From sensor readings to actionable maintenance decision in under a second.

01
INPUT PARAMETERS
Enter 14 engine sensor readings — temperatures, pressures, shaft speeds, and flow/bleed ratios — from the machine's current operational state.
02
ML INFERENCE
A Random Forest, tuned via RandomizedSearchCV and validated with GroupKFold, predicts remaining cycles from the 14 sensor readings.
03
SHAP EXPLANATION
Per-sample SHAP values rank which specific factors are driving this machine's degradation right now.
04
ACT ON INSIGHT
Schedule maintenance before failure. Reduce unplanned downtime, extend equipment life, and cut costs.
